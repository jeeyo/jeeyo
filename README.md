# Hi, I'm Natchanon (Jeeyo) 👋

I'm a technology enthusiast based in Bangkok, Thailand 🇹🇭. I enjoy understanding systems in depth, exploring their boundaries, and finding thoughtful ways to connect technologies across different layers.

My interests span AI, web platforms, self-hosted infrastructure, embedded systems, and microcontrollers. I am particularly drawn to projects that reveal how the underlying technology works and turn experimental ideas into practical tools.

I value continuous learning, careful engineering, and the opportunity to work across abstraction levels—from user-facing applications to constrained devices.

## Areas of interest

- Practical applications of AI and language models
- End-to-end design and implementation of small systems
- Self-hosted services and infrastructure
- Web-platform concepts applied to constrained devices
- Developer tools that simplify recurring problems
